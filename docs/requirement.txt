English: CubeNexus - A Comprehensive Speedcubing Platform for Offline Tournament Management and Online Matchmaking
Vietnamese: CubeNexus - Nền tảng Speedcubing toàn diện quản lý giải đấu trực tiếp và ghép trận trực tuyến
a. Context:
The Speedcubing community is expanding rapidly across both offline World Cube Association (WCA) tournaments and online platforms. Currently, the operational workflow for offline tournaments faces critical bottlenecks: paper-based scoresheets and manual data entry cause significant delays and human errors. Furthermore, there is a lack of software support for complex competition formats like the "Medley" (solving multiple different puzzles sequentially in a single attempt). In the online space, players lack a transparent, real-time 1v1 competitive arena. Existing online events are highly susceptible to cheating due to the absence of camera supervision. Therefore, a comprehensive platform is required to solve both issues: digitizing the offline tournament workflow and establishing a fair online arena that securely synchronizes camera surveillance with a reliable physical timer
Functional requirement :
Guest (Spectator):
● Live Tracking: Access the Web Portal to view real-time Live boards for ongoing offline tournaments without requiring an account.
● Online Ranking: View the Global Top Rank leaderboard of the highest Elo players in the online 1v1 Arena.
Competitor (Player):
● Practice (Smart Timer): Use the Mobile App to practice with system-generated Scrambles (a standardized sequence of moves to shuffle the cube). The system automatically calculates Ao5 and Ao12 (Average of 5/12 solves, excluding the best and worst times) and displays statistical progress charts.
● Offline Tournament Participation: Browse and register for tournaments. Receive a QR Code for check-in at the competition station. Receive Push Notifications calling them to their designated table and view the Live board.
● Online 1v1 Arena (Ranked Matchmaking with Web-Mobile Sync):
oMatchmaking: Users click "Find Match" on the Web. The system pairs them with an opponent of a similar Elo rating.
oPreparation: Upon entering the Virtual Room on the Web, users must enable their Webcam facing their hands/cube (broadcasted via WebRTC to the opponent). A unique QR Session Code is displayed on the Web screen.
oTimer Synchronization: Users scan this QR code using the CubeNexus Mobile App to connect their phone as the dedicated timer for that Virtual Room.
oCompetition: A synchronized Scramble sequence appears on the Web. Both players use their Mobile phones like a Stackmat Timer (placing hands on the screen to start/stop the clock). The first to stop the timer with a solved cube wins. Results instantly sync to the Web to update Elo ratings. A "Report" button is available if an opponent is caught cheating via the webcam feed.
● Asynchronous Online Tournament: Participate in weekly/monthly events by receiving a Scramble, recording a continuous video of the solve, and submitting the video link alongside the time for admin approval.
Judge (At Offline Tournament Stations):
● Verification: Scan the Competitor's QR code using the App to verify their identity and current attempt (Solve 1 to 5).
● Data Entry & Penalties: Read the time from the physical Stackmat Timer on the table and input it into the App. Apply standard WCA penalties: "+2 seconds" (if the cube is one turn away from being solved) or "DNF" (Did Not Finish - the puzzle remains unsolved).
● Medley Event Handling: Evaluate each puzzle in a sequential Medley independently. The system automatically accumulates "+2" penalties into the total time. If any single puzzle in the sequence receives a "DNF", the entire Medley attempt is marked as DNF.
● E-Signature: Display the final inputted time on the device screen for the competitor to verify and digitally sign using the touchscreen before submission.
Tournament Manager / Delegate:
● Tournament Configuration: Create events, select puzzle categories (Traditional or Medley), and set Time Limits or Cut-off times.
● Grouping & Scrambling: Auto-generate competitor Groups/Heats based on previous seed times. Automatically generate cryptographically secure PDF Scrambles (for Medley events, the system generates a sequential "Scramble Bundle" for all puzzles involved).
● Live Operations: Monitor the data entry progress from all judge stations via a real-time dashboard. Resolve disputes and lock results to advance competitors to the next round.
System Admin:
● Platform Management: Approve and grant "Tournament Manager" permissions to clubs or organizations. Manage the Master Data (valid puzzle types, standard penalty rules, and Random State Scrambler parameters).
● Dispute Resolution (Online): Review video reports from the 1v1 Arena or Asynchronous tournaments. Validate results or apply penalties (deduct Elo/ban accounts) for cheating behaviors.
Non-functional requirement:
● Performance: Web-Mobile timer sync latency must be < 200ms via WebSockets. Live leaderboards must update in under 1 second.
● Security: APIs require strict JWT authentication. Offline Scramble PDF files must be password-encrypted.
● Compatibility: The Web application must support major WebRTC-enabled browsers. Mobile apps must be fully compatible with both iOS and Android.

Proposed Solutions:
CubeNexus proposes a closed-loop platform serving two core business domains:
● Offline Tournament Management Ecosystem: Fully digitizes the competition process using a Mobile app for Judges (for data entry, e-signatures, and offline synchronization) and a Web portal for Tournament Managers (for automated grouping, Scramble generation, and real-time leaderboard calculation). It robustly supports both traditional single-puzzle events and Medley events.
● Online Matchmaking Arena: Integrates a 1v1 Ranked Matchmaking system based on Elo ratings. To ensure competitive integrity and prevent cheating, the system utilizes "Web-Mobile Synchronization": Competitors use the Web platform on their PC/Laptop to broadcast their Webcam, while simultaneously connecting their Mobile App to act as a physical Smart Timer, accurately simulating the hand-tapping mechanics used in official competitions.
